OWASP Top 10:2025 — ten mapped examples

Each example illustrates one OWASP category, not complete category coverage. In the local showcase (demo_web.py), four of these are checked by Semgrep demo rules and six by behavioral tests. The webhook reviewer itself uses its 15 production rules and runs no behavioral tests. This page explains them; it runs neither.
